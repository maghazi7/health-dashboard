## Program Structure

**Split**: 5 days on / 2 rest days per week

| Day | Focus | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Upper | Strength |
| Day 2 | Lower | Strength |
| Day 3 | Rest | — |
| Day 4 | Pull | Hypertrophy |
| Day 5 | Push | Hypertrophy |
| Day 6 | Legs | Hypertrophy |
| Day 7 | Rest | — |

**Two training blocks:**

1. **Foundation Block (Weeks 1–5)** — Build movement quality, moderate volume (~20 sets/week), learn RPE calibration
2. **Ramping Block (Weeks 6–12)** — Progressive volume increase up to ~70 sets/week, intensity techniques introduced (drop sets, lengthened partials, myo-reps)

## Guidebook Sections (48-page PDF)

1. **The 6 Transformation Principles**
   - Tension (mechanical tension as the primary driver)
   - Technique (long muscle length bias, stretch-focused movements)
   - Effort (RPE-based autoregulation)
   - Progressive Overload (weight/reps/sets over time)
   - High-Tension Exercises (machine/cable emphasis for hypertrophy)
   - Intensity Techniques (drop sets, lengthened partials, myo-reps — Ramping Block only)

2. **RPE Scale** — 1–10 scale for gauging effort. Foundation Block targets RPE 7–8; Ramping Block pushes to RPE 9–10 on final sets.

3. **Training Split Rationale** — Why Upper/Lower + PPL hybrid; strength days prime the nervous system, hypertrophy days drive volume.

4. **Exercise Substitutions** — Each exercise has 2 listed alternatives. Guidelines for when and how to substitute.

5. **Training Gear Recommendations** — Belt, straps, sleeves, shoes. What's essential vs. optional.

6. **Progress Tracking** — How to log, when to increase weight, deload protocols.

7. **Muscle Anatomy & Exercise Library** — Muscle-by-muscle breakdown (chest, back, shoulders, arms, quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves, abs) with recommended exercises per muscle, long-length emphasis explained per movement.

8. **Nutrition** — Calorie/macro guidance, meal timing around training.

9. **Supplements** — Evidence-based supplement recommendations.

10. **FAQ & References** — Common questions, scientific citations.

## Program Sheets (PDF + Excel)

Each workout day includes:

- **Exercise name** with muscle target
- **Warm-up sets** (prescribed where needed)
- **Working sets × reps** (e.g., 3×8–10)
- **Tracking columns** (Set 1–4 for logging weight/reps)
- **Early Set RPE** and **Last Set RPE** targets
- **Rest period** (seconds)
- **Sub 1 / Sub 2** — Two substitution options per exercise
- **Coaching notes** — Form cues, tempo, common mistakes

The Excel file mirrors the PDF program exactly and is designed for active logging — fill in your weights/reps as you train.

## Key Training Philosophy

- **Long muscle length bias**: exercises chosen to load muscles in the stretched position (e.g., incline curls > standing curls, Romanian DL > leg curls)
- **Machine/cable emphasis**: more stable loading = better isolation for hypertrophy
- **RPE autoregulation**: no fixed percentages — effort is gauged by proximity to failure
- **Progressive volume**: Foundation Block builds the base, Ramping Block pushes total working volume significantly
- **Intensity techniques**: only introduced in Weeks 6–12 once movement quality is established
